# Evidence and claim levels

1. **Workflow demonstrated**: software handoffs execute and outputs parse.
2. **Mechanism complete**: loading, onset, morphology, local damage/opening and
   numerical quality form a reproducible chain.
3. **Conditional comparative result**: designs are compared under frozen,
   disclosed assumptions.
4. **Predictive result**: material/contact parameters, experiments and relevant
   mesh/boundary sensitivities calibrate absolute predictions.
5. **Event-driven rupture/venting result**: a fully cracked face-connected path
   reaches loaded and exterior surfaces, its event time is resolved, and the
   subsequent pressure treatment is stated explicitly.
6. **Fully coupled rupture result**: fluid pressure and structural fracture
   exchange state during the solve rather than through one-way or reduced-order
   feedback.

Do not promote a result to a higher level based only on visual similarity or a
single scalar damage variable.
